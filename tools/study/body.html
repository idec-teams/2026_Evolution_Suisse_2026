Where the lattice comes from

A subunit's penton/hexon identity is not asserted — it is recovered from the deposited symmetry group. The 60 operators contain six 5-fold axes and fifteen 2-fold axes; those give 12 + 30 capsomer centres, and every subunit is assigned to the nearest. The counts come out at exactly five per pentamer and six per hexamer, which is the check that the classification is right.
