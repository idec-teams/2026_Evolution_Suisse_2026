Section 01

The project

Co-encapsulation of dCas9 and sgRNA in QtEncapsulin, selected by continuous directed evolution.

01 Background Encapsulins, prior engineering work, and the gap this project addresses. 02 Design The shell, the cargo handles and the design constraints of the selection. 03 Mechanism How survival is coupled to encapsulation. 04 Results The engineered shell, the selection circuit, what the first assays showed, and where the build goes next. 05 Future work Next experiments and planned continuous-evolution campaign. 06 References Sources cited across the wiki, including verified background literature.

Wild-type QtEncapsulin (QtEnc) loads protein cargo through a C-terminal cargo-loading peptide and has no affinity for nucleic acids. We engineered the shell with a lumenal RNA-binding peptide (λN⁺) and coupled encapsulation of a dCas9–sgRNA complex to survival of E. coli through a kanamycin-resistance selection. Variants of the shell are diversified in continuous culture with MutaT7.

The pages cover the background, the design of the shell and handles, the mechanism of the selection, the results obtained so far and future work. Sources are listed on the references page.
