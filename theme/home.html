How the selection works

{#- Sticky stage. Figures mount into the layers; scroll drives render(t). -#}

{#- Panels. One per act; order here is the order of the story. -#}

01

Diversify

An orthogonal T7 polymerase mutates one locus and nothing else.

MutaT7 fuses a nucleotide deaminase to T7 RNA polymerase, so mutagenesis tracks processively along any cassette placed behind a T7 promoter — and stops at the terminator. We put the encapsulin gene, and only the encapsulin gene, inside that window.

The host genome, replicated by its own high-fidelity machinery, is untouched. Diversity accumulates continuously, in growing culture, without rounds of transformation or any step performed in a tube.

02

Silence

dCas9 parks on the resistance gene and throttles transcription.

A catalytically dead Cas9, guided by an sgRNA into the kanamycin-resistance gene, does not cut — it obstructs. Elongating RNA polymerase collides with the R-loop and is delayed.

The effect is kinetic rather than absolute. Polymerase eventually kicks dCas9 off and completes the transcript, so the locus is throttled rather than switched off, and resistance falls below what the kanamycin in the medium demands.

03

Encapsulate

A shell that captures the repressor restores resistance — and survives.

240 encapsulin subunits assemble into a T=4 icosahedral compartment 42 nm across. Cargo carrying a five-residue cargo-loading peptide docks into a groove on the inner face of every subunit; a grafted λN peptide catches RNA carrying a boxB hairpin.

Sequester dCas9, or its sgRNA, and the gene clears, transcription resumes, and resistance returns. Survival becomes a direct readout of encapsulation.

04

Enrich

Better shells outgrow worse ones, generation after generation.

Because the coupling is continuous, every generation is a selection step. Variants that capture more of the repressor grow faster, and continuous culture integrates that margin over many doublings.

Raising the kanamycin concentration between passages raises the bar for how much must be captured. The population walks toward higher encapsulation efficiency, and the survivors are the library.
