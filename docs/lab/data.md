## Purification step accounting

The most useful thing produced this season is not a single measurement but the
per-step aliquot series: an aliquot retained at every stage of every purification,
with A280 and a gel lane for each. That is what turns "the protein disappeared"
into "the protein is in the pellet after lysis", which is a debuggable statement.

## Sequencing

All constructs were verified by Sanger sequencing of assembly junctions and, where
it mattered, by whole-plasmid sequencing. Alignments are maintained in Benchling.

Negative sequencing results are retained deliberately — the clones that came back
as empty backbone or as an unrelated insert are part of the record of how long
the assemblies took to get right.

## Availability

Raw data will be deposited alongside the report. Sequences of all constructs will
be provided as supplementary information; plasmid maps are maintained in
Benchling.
